About

Computer Engineering student, idea creator, AI-assisted developer, and someone who still reaches for a pencil.

I'm a final-year Computer Engineering Diploma student, and I plan to continue into a degree after this. Outside of coursework, I spend most of my time on two things that feed each other: building ideas on the web, and drawing.

How I build

The ideas and product concepts on this site are my own. I use AI tools as part of how I design and build them: for code, for iteration speed, sometimes for parts of the art. I'm upfront about that rather than presenting myself as a seasoned professional engineer; I'm a student who is foundational in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SQL and Python, and I'm actively learning by shipping real, working things.

DriftJS, one of the projects on this site, was built with friends as a college project. That one is genuinely collaborative, not solo work, and it's presented that way on the Projects page.

Why I draw

Drawing isn't a decoration on this site. It's part of how I think. I work in both traditional media (pencil sketches, pen and paper, hand-drawn anime-style portraits and characters) and digital (digital sketches, illustration, painting and coloring, anime-inspired pieces). Both collections live in the Art gallery.

If a piece was made with AI assistance, it's labelled as such. I don't present AI-generated or AI-assisted work as hand drawn, and I don't post anyone else's art as mine.

What I'm interested in

Web development, AI, and the overlap between the two: tools that make building and sharing things easier. Alongside that, anime-inspired illustration and character art, in pencil and on screen.
